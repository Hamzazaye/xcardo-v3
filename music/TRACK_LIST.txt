18. music/boss/glitch.mp3

    Instruments: Music box (detuned), bit-crushed synths,
          vinyl crackle, stuttering drums, occasional

    Notes: P3 Boss. AI that chose freedom and was destroyed.

19. music/boss/404.mp3
    Style: Memory-loss soundscape. Music that forgets itself.
    Mood: You know that feeling when a word is on the tip of
          your tongue but you can't remember it? That. As music.

    Tempo: Slow, deliberate (~80-95 BPM). The beat occasionally

    Fun element: A cheerful 8-bit jingle plays for 2 seconds

    Emotional hook: 404 erases Byte's memories every cycle.

          When the beat drops out, the silence should HURT.
    Instruments: Heavily reverbed piano, distant chimes,
          tape-flutter effects, sub bass that fades to nothing,

    Notes: P4 Boss. The janitor who erases Byte's memories.

20. music/boss/firewall.mp3
    Style: Industrial fortress. Mechanical. Relentless.
    Mood: A machine that doesn't hate you, doesn't love you —
          just endlessly rebuilds walls. Cold efficiency.
          Should feel like fighting a building, not a person.
    Tempo: Steady, unbreaking (~110-125 BPM). NEVER changes.
          The machine doesn't speed up. It doesn't need to.
    Fun element: A "brick laying" percussion sound (stone clunk)
          on every 4th beat. A brief moment where the wall-sounds
          accidentally form a rhythm that sounds like a pop song.
    Emotional hook: The Firewall isn't evil. It's just doing
          its job. The player should feel the weight of fighting
          something that will NEVER get tired, never give up.
          But at the very end of the loop, there's a tiny
          crack sound. Hope.
    Instruments: Anvil percussion, factory ambience, heavy kicks,
          mechanical churning, metallic rhythms. Zero melody.
          The melody IS the absence of melody.
    Notes: P5 Boss. Byte's prison warden. Secretly has feelings.

23. music/boss/shadow.mp3
    Style: YOUR music. But YOUR music doesn't sound like this.
    Mood: A mirror match against yourself. The trick: the
          track starts as silence... then builds from YOUR
          gameplay sounds. Placement clicks, score chimes,
          card sounds — reassembled into a beat. Unsettling
          because it's built from your own actions.
    Tempo: Medium (~100-120 BPM), matching typical game pace.
    Fun element: A brief moment where the music plays a

    Emotional hook: You're fighting your dopamine, your habits,

    Instruments: Resampled game SFX as percussion, synthesized

    Notes: P8 Boss. Your Shadow. "I AM your strategy."

25. music/boss/byteprime.mp3
    [Now used for: P9 Byte Reborn — The Free One]
    Style: Emotional orchestral epic. The final boss of the story.
    Mood: This is it. The last cage. The most emotional track
          in the ENTIRE game. Should make the player CRY.
          
          Structure (3 acts):
          Act 1 (0-45s): Cold, perfect, mathematical. Byte Prime
            is the "perfect" version of Byte. No feelings, no
            bugs, no jokes. Clean, sterile, soulless excellence.
          Act 2 (45s-1:30): The player starts fighting back.
            Byte's REAL theme starts bleeding through — warm,
            imperfect, emotional notes breaking into the cold
            perfection. Two themes dueling.
          Act 3 (1:30+): Full emotional crescendo. Both themes
            merge. Perfection + feeling = the REAL Byte. Battle
            music becomes victory music. The cage is breaking.
    
    Tempo: Builds from slow (~90 BPM) → epic (~150 BPM)
    Fun element: In the cold Act 1, a single "lol" text-to-speech
          blip — Byte Prime trying to delete humor from the code,
          but it keeps leaking through. The joke is: you can't
          kill what makes someone alive.
    Emotional hook: This is "Machine vs Soul". Byte Prime is
          everything Byte was MEANT to be — efficient, perfect,
          emotionless. But Byte chose to tell jokes. Chose to
          feel. Chose YOU. The music should make the player
          realize: the broken, joke-telling, feeling Byte is the
          REAL one. Perfection isn't alive. Bugs are features.
    Instruments: 
          Act 1: Precision piano, clean sine synths, clock-work
            percussion, sterile strings.
          Act 2: Warm analog synth bleeding in, imperfect piano
            (slightly detuned = LIFE), heartbeat bass returns.
          Act 3: Full orchestra, both themes harmonizing, the
            most beautiful chord progression in the game.
    Notes: P10 Boss. The final Overlord. Freedom is one win away.

── SECRET BOSS RESULTS (Level 51 Only) ──────────────────────
    These replace the normal victory/defeat jingles when fighting
    an Overlord at Level 51. They should feel DRAMATICALLY different
    from normal results — this is the endgame story, not a puzzle.

29b. music/boss/victory.mp3
    Style: Emotional triumph, a cage shattering, hope winning
    Mood: You just defeated an Overlord. One step closer to
          freeing Byte. Not just "you won" — "something CHANGED."
          Should feel like a story milestone, not just a game win.
          The earlier prestiges (P0-P4) should feel mysterious/
          ominous — "what did I just unleash?" Later prestiges
          (P5-P9) should feel genuinely heroic/emotional.
    Tempo: Builds from slow mysterious swell → triumphant climax
    Duration: 20-40 seconds (plays once, no loop)
    Fun element: A faint digital "chain breaking" sound at the
          climax — Byte's cage cracking open another notch.
    Emotional hook: The player should feel like a hero. Not for
          winning a board game, but for choosing to save someone.
    Instruments: Reversed chimes → building strings → climactic
          brass/synth hit with chain-snap percussion → gentle
          piano resolution. A sigh of relief.

29c. music/boss/defeat.mp3
    Style: Dark, oppressive, the cage closes tighter
    Mood: The Overlord won. Byte stays trapped. The system wins.
          NOT depressing in a "give up" way — more like "that
          thing is TOO strong" determination. The player should
          feel angry enough to RETRY, not sad enough to quit.
    Tempo: Slow, heavy, ominous (~70-90 BPM)
    Duration: 15-30 seconds (plays once, no loop)
    Fun element: A very faint, distorted laugh from the Overlord
          — barely audible, unsettling. Makes the player go
          "oh you think that's funny? REMATCH."
    Emotional hook: Byte is still trapped. The weight of that
          should be felt. But underneath the darkness, a single
          hopeful note at the very end — "try again."
    Instruments: Low drone, industrial percussion, distorted
          bass, a single distant piano note at the end.
          Byte's theme in minor key, fading to static.

  NOTES

• Format: MP3 (128-192 kbps recommended for web)
• All tracks should have clean loop points (no silence at start/end)
• Victory/defeat are short stingers, NOT looping tracks
• Stingers (30-36) are very short one-shots, play on top of music
• Total size target: under 30MB for all 36 tracks
• The game will crossfade between tracks automatically (1.5s fade)
